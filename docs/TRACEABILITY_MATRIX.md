## Method

For every one of the 101 IDs in scope: (1) confirmed its owning artifact from the register's
`Repository effect` column; (2) grep-verified the ID's literal string appears in that artifact;
(3) confirmed no *other* artifact's own self-declared `Status:` scope line also claims ownership
of it (cross-domain **mentions** of an ID elsewhere — e.g. a workspace doc citing
`FB-RAT-COM-007` as the shared convention it builds on — are expected practice throughout this
corpus and are not counted as competing ownership; every doc's `Status:` line was read to confirm
it only self-declares its own domain's IDs as *RATIFIED here*).

## Findings

### 1. No dangling IDs, no double-owned IDs (0 real defects)
All 101 in-scope decision IDs were confirmed present, exactly once, in their register-assigned
owning artifact. Every artifact's own `Status:` header line was read and confirms it only
self-declares ownership of its own domain's IDs — cross-domain **mentions** elsewhere (e.g.
`WORKSPACE_KERNEL_SPEC.md` citing `FB-RAT-COM-007`/`FB-RAT-COM-009` as the shared conventions it
builds on, or `DEFERRED_DECISIONS.md`'s `FB-RAT-DEV-011` entry naming `FB-RAT-DEV-008` as its
unblock condition) are expected, intentional cross-references, not ownership conflicts. A raw
whole-corpus grep for each ID (see the gate report) finds most IDs in 2+ files for exactly this
reason; none of the extra hits are self-declared-RATIFIED-here claims.

### 2. Format inconsistency — integration domain cites decision IDs in short form (named gap, not a dangling reference)
All 5 integration-domain ratified docs (`MANUAL_INTEGRATION_GRAMMAR.md`,
`CSAPP_ISSUES_MANIFEST_V1.md`, `ASSAY_REPO_CONTRACT_V1.md`, `IMPORT_RECEIPT_V1.md`,
`INCIDENT_SOURCE_AND_PROOF_CONTRACT.md`) cite their 22 ACCEPTED decision IDs as short-form
`INT-NNN` (e.g. `INT-011`) rather than the full canonical `FB-RAT-INT-NNN` form used
consistently everywhere else in the corpus (all `COM-*`/`WS-*`/`EXE-*`/`AUTH-*`/`DEV-*`/`DIST-*`/
`STR-*`/`PORT-*` citations, and the non-ratified-registers agent's own `FB-RAT-INT-003/004/014/
017/027` citations, all use the full `FB-RAT-` prefix). Content coverage was manually verified
complete — all 22 IDs (`FB-RAT-INT-001,002,005-013,015,016,018-026`) are addressed, in their
correct register-assigned home document, just under the shortened label. This means a literal
`grep -r "FB-RAT-INT-0"` across `docs/ratified/` returns **zero** hits even though the content is
present — a real machine-traceability gap, left unfixed here (a 5-file, ~90-instance rewrite is
outside the "cheap and safe" bar for opportunistic fixes in this gate) and flagged as a named
follow-up. See gate report §4.

Confirmed present on disk, not re-validated or re-listed here (separate WP-1L work package, per
