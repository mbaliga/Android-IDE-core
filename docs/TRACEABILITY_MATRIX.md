## Findings

### 1. No dangling IDs, no double-owned IDs (0 real defects)
All 101 in-scope decision IDs were confirmed present, exactly once, in their register-assigned
owning artifact. Every artifact's own `Status:` header line was read and confirms it only
self-declares ownership of its own domain's IDs — cross-domain **mentions** elsewhere (e.g.
`WORKSPACE_KERNEL_SPEC.md` citing `FB-RAT-COM-007`/`FB-RAT-COM-009` as the shared conventions it
builds on, or `DEFERRED_DECISIONS.md`'s `FB-RAT-DEV-011` entry naming `FB-RAT-DEV-008` as its
unblock condition) are expected, intentional cross-references, not ownership conflicts. A raw
whole-corpus grep for each ID (see the gate report) finds most IDs in 2+ files for exactly this
reason; none of the extra hits are self-declared-RATIFIED-here claims.

### 2. Format inconsistency — integration domain cites decision IDs in short form (named gap, not a dangling reference)
All 5 integration-domain ratified docs (`MANUAL_INTEGRATION_GRAMMAR.md`,
`CSAPP_ISSUES_MANIFEST_V1.md`, `ASSAY_REPO_CONTRACT_V1.md`, `IMPORT_RECEIPT_V1.md`,
`INCIDENT_SOURCE_AND_PROOF_CONTRACT.md`) cite their 22 ACCEPTED decision IDs as short-form
`INT-NNN` (e.g. `INT-011`) rather than the full canonical `FB-RAT-INT-NNN` form used
consistently everywhere else in the corpus (all `COM-*`/`WS-*`/`EXE-*`/`AUTH-*`/`DEV-*`/`DIST-*`/
`STR-*`/`PORT-*` citations, and the non-ratified-registers agent's own `FB-RAT-INT-003/004/014/
017/027` citations, all use the full `FB-RAT-` prefix). Content coverage was manually verified
complete — all 22 IDs (`FB-RAT-INT-001,002,005-013,015,016,018-026`) are addressed, in their
correct register-assigned home document, just under the shortened label. This means a literal
`grep -r "FB-RAT-INT-0"` across `docs/ratified/` returns **zero** hits even though the content is
present — a real machine-traceability gap, left unfixed here (a 5-file, ~90-instance rewrite is
outside the "cheap and safe" bar for opportunistic fixes in this gate) and flagged as a named
follow-up. See gate report §4.

# Part B — loop corpus (added by the WP-1L gate, 2026-08-07)

## Method (differs from Part A — stated explicitly)

Part A's method verified each of 101 IDs against an external register (`inputs/RATIFICATION_
REGISTER.md`, 109 IDs, present on disk during the WP-1 gate). **That `inputs/` pack — and the
`inputs/dual_surface/specs/`, `fonebrew_handoff/*.md`, and `DUAL_RATIFICATION_REGISTER.md` files
the loop corpus's own documents repeatedly cite as their source — do not exist anywhere on disk in
this session** (confirmed by a repo-wide and filesystem-wide search; `HANDOFF_STATE.md` §"Open
threads" implies they were present as session-context during WP-1L's authoring pass but were never
committed to the repository). This is a genuine sandbox/session limit, not a silently skipped step.

Given that, Part B's cross-check is **corpus-internal**: for every `FB-RAT-{LOOP,LBX,PHN,WEB,PKG,
IMP,MKT,RES,CMP,LIN}-*` ID mentioned anywhere under `docs/ratified/loops/`, this gate (1) collected
every document's own "Decision IDs cited in this document" footer (the corpus's self-declared,
per-document ownership list — present in 12 of the 13 WP-1L documents; `LOOP_PHONE_AUTHORING_SPEC
.md` is the one exception, noted in Finding 3 below), (2) collected every inline per-ID `` `FB-RAT-
XXX-NNN` — ACCEPTED/REJECTED/DEFERRED/EXPERIMENTAL/PROPOSED `` ratification statement, (3) cross-
checked the two against each other and against every other document's citations of the same ID,
and (4) for every ID whose declaration named a specific `docs/non_ratified/*.md` register file as
its "true status home," checked whether that ID actually appears as a row in that file. This
proves internal consistency (every ID resolves to a document that actually ratifies it, with no two
documents claiming conflicting ownership) but — unlike Part A — **cannot prove the loop corpus
matches an external 109-ID register**, because that register is not available to check against.

## Findings

### 1. `FB-RAT-LBX-002` — dangling: cited as ACCEPTED corpus-wide, ratified nowhere (real defect, not fixed — see gate report)
`FB-RAT-LBX-002` ("the phone app is the primary, complete, untethered authoring and execution
surface") is cited as already-decided, load-bearing context in **9 of the 13** WP-1L documents
(`AMENDMENTS.md`, `LOOP_DUAL_SURFACE_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `LOOP_ENGINEERING_SPEC_V2.1.md`,
`LOOP_FORK_LINEAGE_CONTRACT.md`, `LOOP_IMPORT_ACTIVATION_CONTRACT.md`, `LOOP_P0_P1_RELEASE_GATES
.md`, `LOOP_PACKAGE_SPEC.md`, `LOOP_PHONE_AUTHORING_SPEC.md`, `LOOP_WEB_STUDIO_SPEC.md`), but no
document's own self-declared "Decision IDs cited in this document" footer claims to ratify it, and
the "canonical home" pointer is internally inconsistent:
- `LOOP_PHONE_AUTHORING_SPEC.md` §"Why this surface is load-bearing" states its canonical home is
  `LOOP_DUAL_SURFACE_ARCHITECTURE.md`.
- `LOOP_DUAL_SURFACE_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s own footer explicitly lists only `FB-RAT-LBX-001` and
  `FB-RAT-LBX-004` as what it ratifies ("No other `FB-RAT-*` ID is ratified by this document") —
  its §1 groups LBX-002 under "phone: `LOOP_PHONE_AUTHORING_SPEC.md`," pointing back the other way.
- `LOOP_WEB_STUDIO_SPEC.md`'s own footer names `LOOP_PHONE_AUTHORING_SPEC.md` as LBX-002's home.
- `LOOP_P0_P1_RELEASE_GATES.md`'s footer cites it as context from **both**
  `LOOP_DUAL_SURFACE_ARCHITECTURE.md` and `LOOP_WEB_STUDIO_SPEC.md` at once.

No document anywhere in this corpus contains a `` `FB-RAT-LBX-002` — ACCEPTED `` statement with the
defining normative quote every other owned ID gets (compare `FB-RAT-LBX-001`'s and `FB-RAT-LBX-004`
's proper treatment at `LOOP_DUAL_SURFACE_ARCHITECTURE.md` §1/§5). This is a genuine dangling
decision-ID reference, not a nitpick — flagged for the owner/next session, not silently invented
here (inventing the missing ratification text would itself violate `FB-RAT-COM-002`'s "stable IDs
are never invented by the document that needs one" rule).

### 2. 14 DEFERRED/EXPERIMENTAL/REJECTED loop-domain IDs cite a `docs/non_ratified/*.md` home that does not contain them
Every one of the 14 IDs below is given an explicit inline citation, in its owning loop document,
naming a specific `docs/non_ratified/*.md` file as "its true status" / canonical home. None of the
14 rows were actually added to that file — confirmed by grep, zero hits for each ID in its named
file:

- **`docs/non_ratified/EXPERIMENTAL_DECISIONS.md`** (missing 3): `FB-RAT-CMP-007`, `FB-RAT-PHN-010`,
  `FB-RAT-PKG-010`.
- **`docs/non_ratified/DEFERRED_DECISIONS.md`** (missing 7): `FB-RAT-IMP-010`, `FB-RAT-LIN-007`,
  `FB-RAT-RES-007`, `FB-RAT-WEB-008`, `FB-RAT-LBX-009`, `FB-RAT-MKT-010`, `FB-RAT-MKT-011`.
- **`docs/non_ratified/REJECTED_ALTERNATIVES.md`** (missing 4): `FB-RAT-PKG-003`, `FB-RAT-WEB-004`,
  `FB-RAT-PHN-003`, `FB-RAT-LBX-005`.

(`FB-RAT-LBX-007` and `FB-RAT-LBX-010` are deliberately excluded from this count: `LBX-007`'s own
text names its source as the external `DUAL_RATIFICATION_REGISTER.md`, not a repo file, and
`AMENDMENTS.md` §5 already documents it as derived from already-ratified WP-1 IDs instead; `LBX-010`
is marked EXPERIMENTAL inline but never itself cites a specific `docs/non_ratified/*.md` path, so
there is no broken promise to check.)

This is a systemic, corpus-wide gap: the WP-1L authoring pass consistently wrote the *promise* that
each non-ACCEPTED loop ID's status is recorded in the corresponding WP-1 register, but the WP-1
register files (written earlier, before the loop domain existed) were never actually updated to add
these 14 rows. Each register file's own stated purpose ("the stable-ID record of decisions...")
implies it should be complete for the whole corpus, not just its authoring work package. This is
**not** a "cheap, safe, single-token fix" like a missing `LICENSE-PENDING` marker — each row needs
real synthesized content (topic, decision text, calibration condition or rejected-alternative
reasoning) matching that register's own schema, which risks misstating the ID's actual content
under time pressure. Left unfixed here and flagged as a named follow-up, same standard as the WP-1
gate's `INT-NNN` citation-format gap.

### 3. `LOOP_PHONE_AUTHORING_SPEC.md` lacks the "Decision IDs cited in this document" closing footer every sibling document carries
A minor structural inconsistency, noted because it made Finding 1's cross-check harder than it
needed to be: all 12 other substantive WP-1L documents end with a "Decision IDs cited in this
document" footer that explicitly separates self-ratified IDs from cited-as-context ones (the
convention this whole Part B method relies on). `LOOP_PHONE_AUTHORING_SPEC.md` — arguably the
document with the *most* IDs to track (11 `PHN-*` plus 2 `LBX-*`) — has no such footer. Its
ownership is still fully recoverable from inline per-section declarations (used above), so this is
not counted as a missing-ID defect, just a format gap worth the next session closing for
consistency with its 12 siblings.

### 4. Registry rule-code cross-check (`LOOP-*` codes, not `FB-RAT-*` decision IDs) — clean
Separately from decision IDs: all 28 `LOOP-*` validation rule codes in `schemas/loops/registries/
loop-validation-rules.v1.json` were cross-checked against every `LOOP-*` code referenced in
`schemas/loops/*.schema.json` and `fixtures/loops/**`. Zero dangling references (every code used in
the new corpus is defined in the registry). Two registry codes are defined but never referenced by
this corpus's schemas or fixtures — not a defect, just unused so far: `LOOP-CAP-002`,
`LOOP-SCHEMA-001`.
