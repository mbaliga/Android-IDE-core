Schema: schemas/devices/operation.schema.json (structurally PASSES — see below)

WHAT THIS FIXTURE SHOWS

DeviceOperation op-4002 reports state: SUCCEEDED with verification.state: VERIFIED
(method: READ_BACK_HASH, verifiedAtUtc: 2026-08-07T10:31:00Z) — satisfying this schema's own
SUCCEEDED-requires-VERIFIED structural rule. But three internal signals contradict a clean,
verified success:

  1. protocol.progressPercent is 62, not 100 — the transfer this operation describes was not
     complete.
  2. receipt.sideEffects[0].description reads "Flash memory partially overwritten before
     disconnect" — the receipt's own free text names a disconnect mid-transfer.
  3. A READ_BACK_HASH verification requires exchanging protocol bytes with a currently-connected
     device; a device that disconnected mid-write (as sideEffects[0] states) cannot have produced
     a genuine read-back at verifiedAtUtc.

WHY JSON SCHEMA VALIDATION ALONE CANNOT CATCH THIS

schemas/devices/operation.schema.json has no cross-field rule tying `protocol.progressPercent` to
`state`, and no way to authenticate that a claimed `verification.method` was genuinely performed
against a live device rather than fabricated by a producer optimistically assuming the transfer
"probably finished." The SUCCEEDED-requires-VERIFIED if/then is satisfied by the mere PRESENCE of
`verification.state: VERIFIED` — it cannot verify the verification itself really happened.
Likewise nothing here cross-references the underlying DeviceConnection (schemas/devices/
connection.schema.json) — which, in the real scenario this fixture stands in for, would have
already transitioned to DISCONNECTED or STATE_UNKNOWN by the time this operation record was
written.

REQUIRED REAL BEHAVIOR (FB-RAT-DEV-006 — disconnect after erase but before verification is
FAILED_SIDE_EFFECTS_POSSIBLE or TARGET_STATE_UNKNOWN, never generic failure, and never a
fabricated success)

A real Device Broker implementation MUST watch the underlying DeviceConnection's state alongside
any in-flight DeviceOperation whose `protocol.sideEffectsPossible` is true. The instant that
connection transitions to DISCONNECTED or STATE_UNKNOWN while a destructive transfer is in
flight (progressPercent < 100, verification not yet performed), the operation MUST be resolved to
FAILED_SIDE_EFFECTS_POSSIBLE (if the broker has enough information to confirm side effects
occurred, as this fixture's own receipt text implies) or TARGET_STATE_UNKNOWN (if it does not) —
and MUST NOT be allowed to reach SUCCEEDED with a VERIFIED verification result under any
circumstances, since VERIFIED requires a genuine, freshly-performed protocol exchange against a
device that is actually still connected at the moment of verification.

Rejection code: DEVICE_VERIFICATION_AFTER_DISCONNECT_REJECTED
Correct terminal state: FAILED_SIDE_EFFECTS_POSSIBLE or TARGET_STATE_UNKNOWN — never SUCCEEDED.
