Why the schema cannot catch this: `graph.mode` and `localHistory` are independent top-level
properties of LoopDraft — this schema's DraftGraph if/then only checks that `mode=REFERENCED`
carries a non-null `referencedDefinitionRef` (satisfied here) and that `mode=EMBEDDED` carries a
non-null `definition`. Nothing in the schema requires `mode` to transition to `EMBEDDED` the
moment `localHistory` starts recording USER-authored MODIFY_PROPERTY operations against the
referenced release's own node IDs (`n2`). LOOP_ENGINEERING_SPEC_V2.1 §11 ('imported packages
remain editable on the phone through a forked draft') and FB-RAT-IMP-006 ('editing an installed
release creates a forked local draft rather than mutating it') together require that the very
first structural edit against a REFERENCED draft produce an EMBEDDED graph.mode with its own
`definition` — a semantic/workflow invariant a JSON Schema document validating one snapshot in
isolation cannot enforce, since detecting the violation requires noticing that mutating
operations accumulated in `localHistory` while `graph.mode` never left REFERENCED.
